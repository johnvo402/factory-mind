# 0005 - Sprint 1 authentication foundation

- **Decision:** Sprint 1 starts with the `Company`, `User`, and `RefreshToken` persistence model only.
- **Reason:** These are the minimum entities required to deliver login, JWT refresh, and company-scoped data in later MVP slices.
- **Boundary:** Manufacturing, knowledge, and AI entities remain out of this migration until their respective sprints.
- **Date:** 2026-07-28
